Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. What This Application Does

TimeTracker is an internal workforce management tool used to record employee clock-in/clock-out times, day off entries, and generate timesheet reports for payroll purposes.

2. Data We Collect

Data TypeWhen CollectedRequired?
Employee nameOn every useYes
Clock in/out timestampsOn each clock eventYes
TimezoneAutomatically on each eventYes
GPS coordinates (lat/lng)Only at clock in/out, only if enabledNo (opt-in)
CommentsWhen manually enteredNo
Device info (user agent)On GPS consent acceptanceOnly with GPS

3. GPS Location Data

Location data is collected only when you explicitly enable the "Send Location (GPS)" toggle in Settings. When enabled:

  • Coordinates are captured only at the moment of clock-in and clock-out events
  • There is no continuous or background tracking
  • You may disable GPS at any time — the toggle is in Settings
  • Previously collected location data remains stored per retention policy

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Payroll processing: Clock times and durations are used to calculate hours worked
  • Location verification: GPS data (if enabled) helps verify shift location for timesheet accuracy
  • Audit trail: Shift edits are logged for compliance and dispute resolution
  • Reports: Aggregated data is used for workforce management dashboards

We do not sell, share, or disclose your data to third parties. Data is accessible only to authorized supervisors and administrators within the organization.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored in a secure cloud database (Supabase) with encryption at rest and in transit
  • API access requires authentication via Bearer token or API key
  • Local data on your device is stored in the browser's localStorage
  • Admin dashboard access requires additional role-based authorization

6. Data Retention

Time and attendance records are retained for 6 years in compliance with New York Labor Law and New Jersey record-keeping requirements. Records are not automatically deleted.

7. Your Rights

  • Disable GPS tracking at any time via the Settings toggle
  • View your own shift history and reports within the app
  • Request access to your personal data from your supervisor or administrator
  • Request correction of inaccurate time entries (subject to approval workflow)

8. California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, GPS coordinates are classified as "sensitive personal information." You have the right to limit the use of this data. To exercise this right, disable the GPS toggle or contact your administrator.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as needed. Material changes will be communicated through the application.

10. Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, contact your direct supervisor or the system administrator.
